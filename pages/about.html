Wisconsin South East Chess Club - Our Journey

Our Story

Wisconsin South East (WISE) Chess Club was founded by Balaji Narayanan and Deepak Gupta, beginning its journey in September 2025 with a mission to provide structured chess coaching for kids and students across Menomonee Falls, Pewaukee, Sussex, Waukesha, Franklin, Milwaukee, Brookfield, and surrounding Wisconsin communities. What began as a growing interest among students in local schools quickly revealed a larger gap—many young players wanted to learn chess seriously and compete in tournaments, but lacked access to consistent chess coaching, structured curriculum, and organized competitive training programs in their area. To address this need, the founders established a local chess training academy and community-based chess club, offering professional guidance, beginner-to-advanced instruction, and a supportive learning environment where students can build strong chess fundamentals and improve steadily over time. Our coaching approach focuses on both skill development and competitive readiness, helping students participate confidently in USCF-rated and WSCF-rated chess tournaments while building long-term improvement through practice, strategy, and mentorship.

⭐ A New Name for a Growing Vision ⭐

WISE Minds - Beyond the Board.

In September 2026, the organization entered a new chapter with the launch of its new brand: WISE Chess Club — Wisconsin South East Chess Club. The WISE brand reflects our continued growth and our vision for the future—expanding access to quality chess education, creating opportunities for competitive play, and building a strong chess community across Southeast Wisconsin. Our founders' commitment to chess education remains at the heart of WISE. We continue to focus on providing students with the coaching, mentorship, and opportunities they need to grow both on and off the chessboard. Proudly built as a Parent–Student Volunteering Initiative, WISE Chess Club continues to serve families across Southeast Wisconsin while working to make structured chess education more accessible to students in our communities.

Policies & Commitments

What We Stand For

These statements guide how we coach, welcome families, and support students in a safe and respectful chess environment.

01

Mission

Build confident chess players through structured coaching, tournament preparation, and positive mentorship.

Mission

Wisconsin South East Chess Club exists to help students grow as chess players and as people. Our mission is to develop essential life skills through chess, including critical thinking, discipline, patience, focus, problem-solving, confidence, and resilience, while giving every student a clear path to improvement. It is also the mission of Wisconsin South East Chess Club to maintain a formal program of instruction to teach the game of chess and to promote and support its educational program through community outreach and local and national partnerships to increase awareness of the educational value of chess.

  • Structured instruction for beginners through advanced students
  • Preparation for club play and tournament competition
  • A supportive environment that builds confidence
02

Non-Discrimination

Every student and family is welcome here.

Non-Discrimination Policy

Wisconsin South East Chess Club provides access to coaching, events, and club participation without discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, ancestry, age, disability, or any other protected status.

We are committed to treating every student and family with fairness, dignity, and respect.

03

Safeguarding

Professional boundaries and age-appropriate interactions are always required.

Policy on Relationships and Interaction With Minors

All coaches, volunteers, and representatives are expected to maintain professional, respectful boundaries with minors at all times. Interactions must remain appropriate, instruction-focused, and consistent with student safety and well-being, and are strictly prohibited from engaging in sexual misconduct, hazing, and harassment.

  • Communication stays club-related and age-appropriate
  • Interactions with minors should be supervised whenever practical
  • No inappropriate personal, romantic, or exploitative relationships are permitted
